About us

Finance software that respects how loan desks really work

KhataBoss was built after years on shop floors—where speed at the counter matters as much as accuracy in the daybook.

Our mission

We help girvi shops, pawnbrokers, and finance houses digitize without losing the trust their customers expect. Every screen in KhataBoss is designed around loan lifecycle, cash movement, and transparent owner reporting.

What we believe

  • Your ledger should never be a guessing game.
  • Staff permissions should mirror real-world roles.
  • Cloud security and local counter speed can coexist.
  • Support should be human, fast, and available when collections run.
