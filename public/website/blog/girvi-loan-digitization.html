How to digitize girvi loan operations without disrupting trust

Customers still expect the same respectful experience at the counter—even when your back office moves to the cloud. The safest path is phased adoption: keep paper receipts optional while staff learn digital loan creation.

Start with loan lists, not everything at once

Mirror your active loan register in software first. When staff see matching totals with the physical khata, adoption accelerates naturally.

Connect daybook early

Every disbursement and collection should hit the daybook the same day. KhataBoss ties loan journals to cash accounts so owners never wonder where money moved.

Use WhatsApp for transparency

Automated due-date reminders reduce disputes and phone calls. Templates keep messaging consistent with your brand.
