Daybook best practices for multi-branch finance teams

A healthy daybook is the heartbeat of any finance business. When branches share one platform, consistency matters more than speed alone.

Close cash before you close the shop

Assign one responsible staff member per shift to reconcile physical cash with KhataBoss cash-in-hand. Variances should be journalized the same day.

Separate loan principal from interest income

Mixed entries hide margin. Use dedicated accounts so trial balance and P&L stay meaningful for owners.

Review owner dashboard weekly

Trends in disbursements, collections, and overdue loans should drive collection strategy—not gut feel alone.
